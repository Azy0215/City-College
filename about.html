ABOUT CITY COLLEGE

San Jose del Monte

VISION

CITY COLLEGE OF SAN JOSE DEL MONTE IS AN INSTITUTION THAT IS DRIVEN TO PROVIDE EXCELLENT EDUCATIONAL
OPPORTUNITIES RESPONSIVE TO THE NEEDS OF THE SOCIETY AND COMMITTED TO CONTINUOUSLY SUSTAIN QUALITY EDUCATION
ANCHORED ON EXEMPLARY LEADERSHIP, DEDICATED SERVICE, ACCOUNTABILITY, AND WITH DEEP SENSE OF PROFESSIONALISM.

MISSION

ENHANCE STUDENTS TECHNICAL AND ACADEMIC COMPETENCE VITAL IN PROVIDING IMMEDIATE AND LONG-TERM SOCIETAL NEEDS;


CREATE NEW PROGRAMS AND SERVICES WITH HIGH LEVEL OF EXCELLENCE AND STANDARDS THUS, CONTRIBUTING TO LOCAL, NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL SIGNIFICANCE;


GENERATE NEW KNOWLEDGE THROUGH A BROAD ARRAY OF SCHOLARLY RESEARCH AND CREATIVE ENDEAVORS;


STRENGTHEN CULTURAL AND SOCIETAL UNDERSTANDING THROUGH LANGUAGE AND THE ARTS, CULTURES, AND THE IMPLICATIONS OF SOCIAL, POLITICAL, ECONOMIC AND TECHNOLOGICAL CHANGE;


DEVELOP A ROBUST COMMUNITY AND INDUSTRY LINKAGES AND PARTNERSHIP;


ENGAGE IN CO-CURRICULAR AND EXTRA-CURRICULAR ENDEAVORS THAT WOULD HARNESS THE HUMAN SKILLS OF ALL MEMBERS OF THE CITY COLLEGE OF SAN JOSE DEL MONTE ACADEMIC COMMUNITY;


PROMOTE CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT BY REGULARLY CONDUCTING EVALUATION ON THE PROGRAMS, SERVICES, SYSTEMS, AND POLICIES THROUGH ACCREDITATIONS AND OTHER INTERNAL AND EXTERNAL QUALITY ASSURANCE MECHANISMS.
