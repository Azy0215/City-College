NO TUITION FEE

CITY COLLEGE OF SAN JOSE DEL MONTE provides FREE TUITION for qualified students.

Students can pursue their education without paying regular tuition fees, subject to the applicable government and school requirements.

NO TUITION FEE DOES NOT NECESSARILY MEAN THAT EVERY POSSIBLE STUDENT EXPENSE IS FREE.
